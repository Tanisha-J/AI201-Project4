Jainism is a selfless religion which preaches non-violence, kindness to all and revolves around the concept that the “Soul is the central point of spiritual discipline.’’ This notion demonstrates that a person’s actions and thoughts are only as good as their surroundings, however, it is up to us to find an influential guide which can help us attain our hoped inner transformation. This core principle led me to reflect on how I can make a positive impact on my community.

Following this idea led me to the doors of my temple, where everyone came to seek forgiveness and salvation, irrespective of their age. I was surprised to find so many kids my age walking around, knowing exactly what prayers to say and helping our elders. This suddenly sparked interest in my religion, and began to go to Sunday school to deepen my understanding of Jainsim and my roots.

Wanting to contribute more and be involved with my community, I became a student volunteer. From getting lunch after Sunday school, I started assisting in making the meals and giving them out to families on the other side of the counter. This helped me be more connected with people and gave me more experience speaking my mother tongue.

I started to devote more hours to my temple, helping organize and do technology work for religious events. However, the task that inspired me the most was making food for the homeless. It allowed me to tap into my compassion for others and face the reality that not everyone has the privilege of my life with food on my table and a roof over my head.

Through the efforts of spending time around my culture, I hope to inspire others to really connect with their loved ones and their community.
