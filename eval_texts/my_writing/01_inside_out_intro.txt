Between 2019 and 2024, EQ has decreased by 5.79% globally, according to the National Library of Medicine (Freedman).  EQ, or emotional quotient, is defined as emotional intelligence which “is the ability to blend thinking and feeling to make optimal decisions” (Medina). As shown in the film, Inside Out, we deep dive into a child’s mind and learn how 5 core emotions work together to make decisions, experience things, and understand life, displaying the importance of EQ. Having a high EQ is just as important as having a high IQ because they are complementary aspects of intelligence. Emotional intelligence is a life long skill and it is important to learn how to understand and manage emotions at a young age. Inside Out, released in 2015 by Pixar, is an accurate depiction of how children should handle emotions by emphasizing the acceptance of both positive and negative emotions instead of suppression, and the importance of expressing all emotions in moderation.
