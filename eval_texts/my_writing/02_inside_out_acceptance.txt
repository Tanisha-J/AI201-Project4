Accepting and processing all emotions is crucial to mental well being. Many assume that being optimistic is the only way we should be, however, all of our emotions are in us and are necessary. “Inside Out” focuses on 5 emotions: Joy, Sadness, Fear, Disgust and Anger which are all in control of the control board in Riley’s head, but it's mostly Joy. The main character, Riley, is an 11-year old girl who is battling between emotions after moving across the country.

In her mind are 5 core memories which are yellow orbs, focusing on Joy’s interpretation and influence on the memory. Sadness touches the memory orb and it starts to turn blue, which makes Joy panic and immediately take it away but realizes that the memory now has a touch of blue. She makes Sadness stand in a circle drawn by chalk, instructed to not move or touch anything, inferring that she cannot make Riley sad again because she needs to be happy but Sadness continues to feel anyway. This conveys that emotions such as sadness cannot be contained into a circle and shut out because they will find a way to come out, and it increases the chances of it coming out in a bad situation. Another example of this is a conversation between Joy, Sadness, and Bing Bong, Riley’s old invisible friend. Bing Bong is upset that his rocket is gone into Memory Dump since Riley forgot it and Sadness goes to comfort him, however, Joy tries to stop this too.
